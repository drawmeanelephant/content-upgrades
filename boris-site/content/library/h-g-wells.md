# H. G. Wells

- Born: 1866
- Died: 1946
- Known for: *The Time Machine*, *The War of the Worlds*, *A Modern Utopia*

[[e-m-forster]] wrote "The Machine Stops" as a reaction to one of Wells's
early utopias.
