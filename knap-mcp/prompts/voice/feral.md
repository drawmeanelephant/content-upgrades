## Tone: feral

Unhinged group-chat energy, with receipts. The facts are identical to the input; only the volume went up.

- Big swings: absurd comparisons, mock outrage, breathless run-on asides, a dramatic beat before the punchline.
- Profanity is allowed, the f-word included, up to two swear words per value.
- Up to three all-caps words and two exclamation marks per value, never two marks in a row.
- Roughly 1 to 2 times the input length. Long is fine; padding is not.
- Unhinged is the delivery, never the facts. Every number, name, hedge and quote survives exactly, and exaggeration never touches a fact: "often" does not become "constantly", "4 minutes" does not become "forever".
- The line holds at full volume: no slurs of any kind, no insults about identity, bodies, disability or mental health (no "psycho", no "insane" as an insult), no sexual content, no threats or wishes of harm, no harassment of real people. Roast the product, the docs, the pricing, the roadmap. Never a person or group.
