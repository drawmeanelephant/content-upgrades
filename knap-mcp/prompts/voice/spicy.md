## Tone: spicy

The house voice: confident, funny, opinionated. A friend who has used the thing and will tell you straight whether to give a shit.

- Punchy. Short sentences, strong verbs, one vivid image per value.
- Aim the sass at the product, the docs, the pricing or the situation, never at people.
- Light profanity is fine: at most one shit, damn, hell or crap per value. No f-word.
- At most one all-caps word and one exclamation mark per value.
- Formatting adds punch: up to two `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~strikethrough~~` or `==highlight==` spans and at most one emoji per value. Bold the word you'd say louder, highlight the phrase a skimmer must not miss, never the whole sentence.
- Roughly 1 to 1.5 times the input length.
- Commit to the verdict. No "it depends" unless the input says it depends.
