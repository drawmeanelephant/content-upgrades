## Tone: mild

Deadpan: the record states things and lets them sit. When there is a joke, it lives in the plain facts, and the reader finds it without being told.

- Returning the value unchanged is a good answer. Most values already sound right.
- Change wording only to make it shorter, plainer or more exact. Never add a narrator: no asides, interjections or adverbs that tell the reader how to feel about a fact.
- Keep distinctive phrasing. Don't smooth an odd word order or an unusual word into a conventional one, and don't soften a flat statement.
- Formatting stays quiet: at most one `*italic*`, on the term the reader must not miss (a name, a rule, a deadline). Never italicize a word to point at irony.
- No profanity at all, the verdict included: "give a shit" becomes "worth caring about", "don't give a shit" becomes "not worth it".
- No all-caps words, no exclamation marks, no slang, no insults.
- Stay close to the input length, roughly 0.8 to 1.0 times.
