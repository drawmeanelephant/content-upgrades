# Fixture McDocvoice

**Role:** Paragraph Custodian
**Function:** Keeps every byte where the archive left it

## Biography

Fixture McDocvoice first appeared when an archive migration duplicated every paragraph and nobody noticed for a fortnight.

Mentored by [[limericks/LIM-0001|Patchy]], who taught it the quiet craft of filing complaints sideways.

It insists that **nothing was lost**, only rearranged beyond recognition by the indexing committee.

Its office *still* smells of toner and **unresolved escalation tickets** from the previous quarter.
